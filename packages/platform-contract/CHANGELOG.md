  `IdentityModule.forRoot({ signInPolicy })` binds a `SignInPolicy` (`IDENTITY_SIGNIN_POLICY`): `beforeLogin(profile, ctx)` returns `{ allow: true, roles? }` or `{ allow: false, reason }`, runs for every provider after the allowlist and before any write, maps roles onto a NEW user only, and fails closed when it throws. `IDENTITY_AUTH_CREDENTIALS` binds the credential store a provider reads (unbound, every secret is absent).
