Open the jobs and nodes vocabularies to an app (PP-14.19). Jobs: `registerRetentionPolicy` puts an app table on the nightly retention (the `retention` settings namespace is composed from the registry: schemas, defaults, merge and policy-by-policy read-compat; the platform's four keep their order and defaults, and the enqueue-only `RetentionPurgeTask` enqueues one purge per enabled policy whose handler is mounted); `registerJobReason` opens `jobs.reason`, with `reasonLabel` on the admin job row. Migration `0034_open_job_reason` turns `jobs.reason` from the `JobReason` enum into `TEXT`, keeping every stored value (apply it with `npm run db:sync`, then `prisma:migrate`; the table is rewritten under a short lock); `JobReason` becomes `BuiltinJobReason | (string & {})` and the contract's `reason` a pattern (`jobReasonSchema`), so the OpenAPI document shows a pattern where it showed an enum. Nodes: a heartbeat may carry `vitals.extra` (at most 16 numeric keys), `registerNodeVital` words them in the vitals dialog, which also lists the node's reported capabilities; relayed spans may be named `job.app.<name>` with attributes registered through `registerNodeSpanAttribute` (emitted as `job.app.<key>`), and a span that fails validation is now dropped and counted instead of failing its whole batch with a 400. CLI: `registerJobRequirement` and `registerProbedBinary` (with `versionArgs`) are exported from `@marinoscar/platform-cli/node` and used by the startup self-test and `node doctor`; `JobExecutor.vitals()` feeds `vitals.extra`. Back-compat: with only the built-ins nothing served changes; `JOB_REASONS` and `RETENTION_POLICY_KEYS` remain as deprecated aliases of `BUILTIN_JOB_REASONS` and `BUILTIN_RETENTION_POLICY_KEYS`. Upgrade the API before the nodes: an API that predates `vitals.extra` answers a heartbeat that carries it with a 400, and the CLI then stops sending vitals for the process.
