OAuth server: client identity and the client trust policy (PP-15.5, #1183). Clients identify themselves three ways, all behind the `ClientResolver` seam: a Client ID Metadata Document (an HTTPS `client_id` URL, fetched at `/authorize` only through the shared egress guard with a pinned GET, no redirect, 5 s, 64 KiB, cached 5 minutes to 24 hours, stale copy on a failing refetch), dynamic registration (`POST /api/oauth/register`, RFC 7591, idempotent for identical public registrations, 10 per hour per address) and pre-registered clients (`/api/admin/oauth/clients`: list, create, patch, rotate-secret, delete, every write audited; disabling revokes the client's grants with `client_disabled`). The trust policy (`allowedClientHosts`, `allowAnyClientHost`, `allowLoopbackClients`; a CIMD client's redirect hosts must be its own or allowed) runs at `/authorize`, `/register` and `/token`; a refused host is the error page `client_not_allowed` (with `host`) and at most one `oauth:client.refused` audit row per host per hour. `registration_endpoint` and `client_id_metadata_document_supported` are advertised exactly while their settings are on. New permissions `oauth_clients:read` and `oauth_clients:write` (system, `admin`). New daily server-only job `oauth.client.cleanup` (a `dynamic` client with no grant and no use for `dynamicClientUnusedDays` is deleted). The webhooks egress guard moved to `core/egress` (re-exported by webhooks under the same names, no behaviour change) with a new pinned, bounded `PinnedGetTransport`. Contract: the registration and admin client shapes, and the error codes `invalid_redirect_uri` and `invalid_client_metadata`.
