OAuth server: conformance suite, test kit, Doctor checks, token cleanup, audit and metrics (PP-15.8, #1186). New conformance suite `oauth-server` (`suites.oauthServer`, registered by importing `@marinoscar/platform-api/identity/testing`) with ten checks: `scope-permissions-registered`, `resource-scopes-registered`, `resource-prefix-exclusive`, `token-confinement`, `scope-narrowing`, `session-only-routes`, `no-roles-only-admission`, `challenge-on-401`, `metadata-shape` and `secret-egress` (an app passes evidence from a real flow, or a written skip). Test kit in `identity/testing`: `mintOAuthTestToken`, `createOAuthTestClient`, `pkcePair`, `runOAuthAuthorizationCodeFlow`, `exchangeOAuthCode`. Five read-only Doctor checks in the `auth` category: `oauth.public_url`, `oauth.well_known` (through the egress guard), `oauth.client_policy`, `oauth.resources`, `oauth.token_cleanup`. New hourly server-only job `oauth.token.cleanup` (expired or decided requests, consumed or expired codes older than 1 hour, access tokens expired over 24 hours, refresh tokens expired or rotated over 7 days, tokens of grants revoked over 30 days, grants revoked over 90 days; batched). New audit action `oauth:settings.update` (written in the settings transaction). Four counters through `IDENTITY_METRICS` (`OAUTH_APP_METRICS`, register them with `registerPlatformHostAppMetrics`): `app.oauth.authorizations`, `app.oauth.tokens.issued`, `app.oauth.token.refusals`, `app.oauth.client.registrations`, with closed labels only; `IdentityMetrics.add` takes optional attributes. `@Trace` spans `oauth.authorize`, `oauth.decision` and `oauth.token` carry `oauth.client.kind` and `oauth.resource.id`. The starter mounts the sub-slice (off at runtime) with an example scope and resource in `src/platform/oauth-example.ts`.
