Storage: per-row provider routing and the resumable-upload seams. `StorageProviderRouter` resolves the provider recorded on a row (`storage_provider` + `bucket`) instead of the active one, built from `StorageConfigService.resolveDriver(id)` (a non-active driver's settings and secrets), so an administrator who switches drivers can still download, delete, process and finish every object written before the switch; the objects API, post-upload processing, avatars and the stale-upload sweep use it. `StorageProvider` gains optional `partUploadMode` (`presigned` | `proxied`), `uploadPart` and `listUploadedParts` (`S3StorageProvider` implements `ListParts`). New routes: `POST /api/storage/objects/:id/upload/part-urls` (more part URLs, at most 100 per call) and `PUT /api/storage/objects/:id/upload/parts/:partNumber` (an API-proxied part for a driver with no presigned URLs: streamed, never buffered, bounded to the exact part size, S3-style empty body and quoted `ETag`; call `registerStoragePartBodyParser` in `main.ts`). `upload/init` also returns `partUploadAuth` (`none` | `bearer`); upload status reports the parts the backend holds; `complete` answers `409 UPLOAD_PARTS_MISSING` (`details.partNumbers`) or `409 UPLOAD_SESSION_INVALID` instead of a `500` or a silently partial object. Refusals carry `details.reason` (`UPLOAD_NOT_ACTIVE`, `PART_OUT_OF_RANGE`, `PART_SIZE_MISMATCH`, `PRESIGNED_PARTS_REQUIRED`, `RAW_BODY_REQUIRED`). The driver kit gains `multipartList` and `proxiedPart` scenarios and `storageConformanceSuite` a `storage-routing` case. Contract: `partUploadAuth`, `getPartUrlsSchema` / `getPartUrlsResponseSchema`, `UPLOAD_ERROR_REASONS`. Web: `StorageObjectsClient.partUrls`. Copy-only migration between providers is a follow-up.
