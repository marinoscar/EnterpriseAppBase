Add `@marinoscar/platform-api/identity` and `/identity/testing` (#727): the identity slice moved from the reference app. `IdentityModule.forRoot({ imports, defaultOrgRole, initialAdminEmailEnv, enableTestAuth })` mounts sign-in and tokens (JWT sessions, refresh-token rotation, personal access tokens, worker-node credentials, Google sign-in), users, the allowlist, the device flow, organizations and tenancy, two server-only cleanup job types (`auth.token.cleanup`, `device-auth.code.cleanup`, unchanged) and the `auth.*` / `tenancy.mode` Doctor checks. Stable: the route-access decorators and guards (`@Auth`, `@Public`, `@Roles`, `@Permissions`, `@CurrentUser`, `@CurrentPrincipal`, `@CurrentOrg`, `@AuthCredential`, `JwtAuthGuard`, `RolesGuard`, `PermissionsGuard`), the sign-in error codes, the tenancy parser and the role and permission declarations. Experimental: the seams (`registerAuthProvider`; the host ports `IDENTITY_NOTIFIER`, `USER_DEFAULTS`, `IDENTITY_PROFILE_IMAGES`, `IDENTITY_JOBS`, `IDENTITY_METRICS`, `IDENTITY_NODE_CREDENTIALS`, `IDENTITY_EVENT_BUS`; the `identity.user.created`, `identity.membership.changed` and `identity.org.switched` events; `identityConfiguration()`), the `identity` conformance suite and `createStubIdentityHost`. A missing `JWT_SECRET` is now a boot error (`requireJwtSecret`): no fallback secret ships. Routes, permissions, metrics and the OpenAPI document are unchanged.
