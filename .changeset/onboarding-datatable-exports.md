Close the small vocabularies of onboarding, the DataTable and exports (PP-14.20, #938).

Onboarding. The audience vocabulary is open: `registerOnboardingAudience({ id, label, matches(principal, ctx) })` of `@marinoscar/platform-api/onboarding`, with `user`, `admin` and the new `org_admin` (the holder of the `org_admin` role of an active organization) registered through it. `OnboardingAudience` is `Builtin | (string & {})`; `onboardingAudienceSchema` is a string pattern (the OpenAPI `audience` enum becomes a pattern); `ONBOARDING_AUDIENCES` (`admin`, `user`) is deprecated for `BUILTIN_ONBOARDING_AUDIENCES`. `GET /api/onboarding` gains an optional `audiences` record with the block of every other audience the caller matches and that has a step for them, absent otherwise; `user` and `admin` keep their rules (the engine ignores a rule registered for those ids). `evaluateOnboarding` takes optional `audiences` and `audienceContext`, the `onboarding` conformance suite derives the `org_admin` block and flags a step naming an unregistered audience, and the web provider keeps the `audiences` blocks and their skip overlay.

DataTable. `registerDataTableFilterType({ type, operators, Editor, matches, coerce?, format? })` of `@marinoscar/platform-web/datatable/headless`; the five built-ins register through it, `FilterOperator` and `DataTableFilterType` are open (`BuiltinFilterOperator`, `BuiltinDataTableFilterType`), `DataTableColumn.filter = { type, editor?, operators? }` declares a column's filter, and `matchesFilters` filters rows on the page with the type's rules. `DataTableStoredLayout` gains `extra` (bounded plain JSON, 8 KiB, 32 keys, 8 levels) and `savedViews` (at most 20), validated by the user-settings API on write (`dataTableExtraSchema`, `dataTableSavedViewSchema`, a 32 KiB cap per table entry; the user-settings OpenAPI gains both fields) and sanitised on read; `useDataTableLayoutPrefs` gains `extra`, `setExtra`, `savedViews`, `saveView`, `applyView` and `deleteView`, and `reset` keeps the saved views. `DataTable` takes `slots={{ Toolbar, BulkBar }}`. The filter-model operator unions of the jobs, nodes, identity and db-backup slices gain `(string & {})`. A table that uses none of this renders the markup it did.

Exports. `registerExportWriter({ attachTo: '*' | string[], columnTypes? })` offers a writer on sources that do not list it, `ExportSource.columnTypes` and `acceptsAttachedWriters` gate and pin it, and `writersFor` returns the listed formats then the attached ones (platform writers first). The platform sources list no formats: their formats are the writers attached to them, so json, csv and xlsx are unchanged and an app's writer follows. A writer that cannot write a column type fails the export before any row (`guardWriterColumns`). `ExportColumn.meta` (flat scalars, at most 16 keys) carries a custom writer's own typing; the built-in writers never write it. `ExportsList` takes `slots={{ Columns, RowActions }}`. A source may now list an empty `formats`; `exports` conformance flags a writer attached to an unknown source.
